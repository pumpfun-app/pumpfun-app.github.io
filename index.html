What is Pump.fun

What Pump.fun is

A memecoin launchpad on Solana

Pump.fun is a launchpad on Solana where anyone can create a memecoin and where other people can buy or sell it straight away. There is no order book and no liquidity pool to seed first: each token launches on a bonding curve, a formula that sets the price from how much of the token has already been bought.

From curve to DEX

Buyers push a token up its curve and the price rises; sellers move it back down. When a token fills its curve it graduates, which means its liquidity moves into a DEX pool and it trades there like any other Solana token.

Treat it as speculation

Most memecoins have no product, no roadmap and nothing supporting their price. This page is an interface for trading them from your own wallet, not a recommendation to do so.

Curve vs DEX

Bonding curve vs DEX pool

A token lives in two stages. Knowing which one you are in tells you what sets the price and how much a large trade can move it.

In practice

On the curve you can usually buy or sell instantly because the contract is always the other side. After graduation the token is just another pool-traded asset, and thin pools can move sharply on small orders.

How to trade

How to trade on Pump.fun

Quote and slippage

Before you sign, the interface shows the expected amount you receive, the price impact and a slippage tolerance. Memecoin prices can move between the quote and the moment your transaction lands, so a low tolerance may cause a failed trade.

Step by step

  1. Connect your Solana wallet and make sure it holds SOL for the trade and for fees.
  2. Choose the token you want and whether you are buying or selling.
  3. Enter an amount and review the quote, price impact and slippage tolerance.
  4. Sign the transaction in your wallet; it settles on Solana in a short time.
  5. Check the result in your wallet and keep an eye on the position, since prices can change quickly.

Only trade an amount you are fully prepared to lose.

Fees & gas

What trading on Pump.fun costs

Network fees in SOL

Every transaction on Solana is paid in SOL, including a small base network fee and an optional priority fee that can help it land faster when the network is busy. See how Solana fees work.

Trading cost

A trade also carries price impact, your slippage tolerance, and any platform or creator fee built into the quoted amount. The quote you confirm already reflects these, so the amount shown is what you should expect to receive.

Keep SOL in reserve

Receiving a new token can require a small SOL deposit for the token account that holds it. Keep a little SOL spare, or later buys, sells and exits may fail.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. The interface routes and quotes; it never holds your funds or keys.

Memecoin risk is high

  • Creators can sell large holdings at any time, and a price can fall to near zero without warning.
  • Bots and snipers often buy first, so early buyers can be at a disadvantage.
  • Verify the token address, because copycats reuse names and images.
  • Never sign a transaction you do not understand or share your seed phrase with anyone.
  • Smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Pump.fun FAQ

What is Pump.fun?

Pump.fun is a Solana memecoin launchpad where tokens start on a bonding curve and can be bought or sold at once. When a token fills its curve it graduates to a DEX pool. This page is an independent interface for trading from your own wallet.

What is a bonding curve?

A bonding curve is a formula that sets a token's price from how much of its supply has been sold. Buying pushes the price up and selling pushes it down, with the curve contract acting as the counterparty. It means there is no separate liquidity pool during this stage.

What does it mean when a token graduates?

A token graduates when it fills its bonding curve. Its liquidity then moves to a DEX pool, and from that point the price is set by pool trades instead of the curve.

Which token pays the fees?

Fees are paid in SOL, the native token of Solana. That covers the network fee, any priority fee you choose, and the platform or creator fees reflected in the quote. Keep some SOL in your wallet even if you hold other tokens.

Can I lose everything I put into a memecoin?

Yes. Memecoins are extremely volatile, many have no lasting value, and a price can drop to near zero quickly. Only use money you can afford to lose entirely.

Does this site hold my funds or keys?

No. It is a non-custodial interface: you connect your own Solana wallet and sign each transaction yourself. It has no access to your keys and cannot move your funds.
